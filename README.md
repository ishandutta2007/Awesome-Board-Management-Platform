# Awesome-Board-Management-Platform

## Top Board Management Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Board Governance, Meeting Management & Director Collaboration*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Board Management**. These tools help boards of directors, committees, and governance teams manage board meetings, distribute board packets, track decisions, and collaborate securely.



**Examples** include Diligent Boards, BoardEffect, Boardable, Convene, OnBoard, BoardPAC, Board Intelligence, Aprio, Digital Boardroom, and BoardPro (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with active projects for self-hosting, custom governance workflows, and transparent board operations — ideal for nonprofits, associations, and governance teams building vendor-independent board management solutions. Note that the open-source ecosystem for full board management platforms remains limited, as most governance software is commercial. However, GRC (Governance, Risk & Compliance) platforms and collaborative meeting tools provide strong foundations for board governance workflows.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Probo](https://github.com/getprobo/probo)**  

  Self-hostable governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) platform built for engineering and security teams, ISC licensed. Covers the full GRC lifecycle: risk identification, control tracking, vendor risk, data privacy, access reviews, audit programs, and document approval workflows. Every entity is accessible through a web console, a CLI (`prb`), a Model Context Protocol (MCP) API, and a GraphQL API. Features 270+ MCP tools for AI agent interaction, policy-based RBAC, immutable audit logs, electronic document sign-off workflows, and evidence chains. Run on your own infrastructure with Docker. While not a board management platform per se, it provides the governance, risk, and compliance foundations that boards require for oversight and decision-making. The document management, approval quorums, and electronic signatures capabilities are directly applicable to board resolutions and policy approvals .



- **[Cairn](https://github.com/frousselet/cairn)**  

  Open-source Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) platform, AGPL licensed, designed for self-hosting. Track compliance with ISO 27001, GDPR, NIS2, and other frameworks; run structured risk assessments (ISO 27005, EBIOS RM); and handle security incidents with evidence trails. Features governance module covering scopes, strategic issues, stakeholders, objectives, SWOT, roles, and activities — the ISO 27001 clause 4 context. Includes a unified tasks board, management reviews, and PDF/DOCX/PPTX/XLSX reports. Built for the audit with full change history, versioning, scope-based tenancy, role-based permissions, and passkey login. Bilingual (English and French) with optional AI assistant that cites real records. Django 5.2, PostgreSQL 16, and Bootstrap 5.3 stack with no JavaScript build step. The governance module and reporting capabilities provide a strong foundation for board oversight workflows .



- **[Unicis Platform CE](https://github.com/UnicisTech/unicis-platform-ce)**  

  Modern, all-in-one Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) solution designed for privacy, security, and compliance teams, Apache 2.0 licensed. Open-source alternative to Vanta and Drata with full control, flexibility, and transparency. Features standards and frameworks mapping (ISO 27001, GDPR, SOC2, NIST), mapping matrix, export/import Statement of Applicability (SoA), REST API with OpenAPI 3.0 spec, API key authentication, audit logs, roles and permissions, and WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility. Includes live domain health dashboard showing Data Protection, Cybersecurity, and Risk Management status. While primarily a GRC platform, its governance and compliance capabilities align with board oversight requirements for organizational risk and compliance posture .



- **[EasyBoard](https://vibecode.law/showcase/easyboard-547268)**  

  The first AI-native board management platform, open source and self-hosted. Upload draft minutes, resolutions, or financial reports — the AI classifies the document, extracts key data, and proposes next actions (create a meeting, start a vote, assign a task). The Secretary reviews and approves; nothing executes without human approval. Features 7 document types recognized (draft minutes, resolutions, financial reports, evidence/deliverables, meeting agendas, legal opinions, general documents), full voting workflow with 4 options and custom approval rules (unanimous, majority, two-thirds, quorum), secret ballot, minutes lifecycle with SHA-256 integrity hashes, per-document access control for conflict-of-interest recusal, 4 role types (Secretary, Board Member, Management, Observer), natural language command bar, full audit trail, and self-hosted data sovereignty. Built with Replit and Claude by a practising lawyer with zero engineering background. Proof of concept showing how far AI-assisted development can take practitioner-designed governance tooling .



- **[Parabol](https://github.com/ParabolInc/parabol)**  

  Open-source collaborative workspace for running structured, effective, and inclusive team meetings, Apache 2.0 licensed. Supports retrospectives, sprint planning, standup meetings, check-ins, agile estimation, and collaborative documentation — all in real time with a GraphQL API. Easily self-hosted on your own infrastructure and works in air-gapped environments with no external dependencies. Runs on Node.js + PostgreSQL + Valkey. Features Pages (real-time collaborative documents powered by Yjs) scoped to teams, meeting outcomes automatically captured, and integrations with Jira, GitHub, GitLab, Linear, Azure DevOps, Slack, Mattermost, and Google Calendar. While designed for agile teams rather than boards, its structured meeting facilitation, decision capture, and collaborative documentation capabilities are applicable to board committee meetings and governance working sessions .



- **[Tapiz](https://github.com/juanfran/tapiz)**  

  Self-hosted, web-based collaborative board designed to assist teams with online meetings and projects. Features real-time collaboration, sticky notes, freehand drawing, polling, estimations, and voting. Built with Angular, NgRx, Fastify, Drizzle ORM, PostgreSQL, TRPC, Lucia, and Nx. Docker deployment for self-hosting. The polling and voting features make it suitable for simple board decisions or committee brainstorming sessions, though it lacks formal governance workflows .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **OpenProject** — Open-source project management with meetings module, agenda management, and minutes. Not board-specific but provides structured meeting workflows .

- **Focalboard** — Open-source project board tool with Kanban, table, gallery, and calendar views. Suitable for task tracking and committee action item management .

- **QuickRetro** — Free, lightweight, open-source web app for conducting remote retrospectives with real-time collaboration, multiple languages, and Docker deployment. AGPL-3.0 licensed .



**Frameworks for building custom board management solutions**: Combine **Probo** or **Cairn** for governance, risk, and compliance foundations including document management, approval workflows, and audit trails . Use **EasyBoard** for AI-native board meeting workflows with document classification, voting, and minutes management . Leverage **Parabol** for structured meeting facilitation and decision capture in board committees . For simple collaborative sessions, **Tapiz** provides real-time sticky notes, polling, and voting . Note that true enterprise board management platforms with board packet distribution, director portals, and meeting minute approval workflows remain primarily commercial territory; open-source stacks provide strong GRC foundations, meeting facilitation, and governance workflow components that require integration for complete board management.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Board management tools handle sensitive governance and fiduciary information. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, access controls, and backup procedures.

- Open-source board management platforms are less mature than commercial offerings. Evaluate gaps in functionality, board packet distribution, and director portal features before deployment.

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong GRC foundations and meeting facilitation tools, but full enterprise board management with secure document distribution, voting workflows, and director engagement features remains primarily a commercial offering.
